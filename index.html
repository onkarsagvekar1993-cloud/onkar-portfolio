About

The person behind

I'm a Senior Motion Graphics Designer based in Mumbai with 6+ years of experience creating video content that works — across industries, audiences, and platforms.

I've worked with India's most recognizable brands — Dabur, HUL, Godrej, Brut India, Candid Powder, Axis Mutual Fund and more. I understand what each brand needs and deliver it without missing deadlines.

Right now I'm leading a team of 2 designers at First Economy while staying hands-on with production. I'm also one of the earlier adopters of AI tools in creative workflows — actively using RunwayML, Magnific, Higgsfield, Kling, Veo in real client work.
